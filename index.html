          Formats Percival reads
          Contacts
          Dense square matrix; sparse triplets i j count (GRAAL, hicstuff, HiC-Pro .matrix, with or without header); bedgraph2 chr1 s1 e1 chr2 s2 e2 count (cooler dump); 4DN .pairs, binned at the bin size above.
          Bins
          BED of bins (HiC-Pro _abs.bed); GRAAL / hicstuff fragments_list.txt.
          Sequences
          FASTA, or just lengths as .chrom.sizes / .fai. With a FASTA, the GC track and sequence export are available.
          Layouts
          AGP from an earlier scaffolding (YaHS, SALSA, 3D-DNA), or a Percival session file. Added with a map, or on their own to rearrange the map that is open.

          The score

          Each contact count is modelled as Poisson: between bins i and j of the same scaffold, separated by s base pairs, the expected count is bi bj P(s); across scaffolds it is bi bj Ptrans. The biases b come from iterative correction (ICE) and P(s) is the contact decay measured on the starting layout, made non-increasing and extended as a power law. The headline number is how much the log-likelihood of all contacts has risen since the start; every hint is ranked by the exact change it would make.

          This is the likelihood GRAAL and instaGRAAL sample with MCMC, here evaluated exactly after each manual edit. The decay is frozen so scores stay comparable; refit it from the Decay tab once the layout is mostly right.
